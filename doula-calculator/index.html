How this is calculated
Total service cost
Total cost = Concierge fee + (Total visits × Copay per visit)
Total visits = Pre-birth visits + Post-birth visits
Calendar year split
The concierge fee and all pre-birth visits fall in the year of your due date. Post-birth visits are assumed every 2 weeks starting on the due date. Any that extend past December 31st are counted in the following year, when your deductible resets.
Post-birth visits in Year 1 = min(Post-birth visits, ⌊Weeks remaining in year ÷ 2⌋)
Post-birth visits in Year 2 = Post-birth visits − Year 1 post-birth visits
Your out-of-pocket cost (per year)
Runs separately for each calendar year. In Year 2, the deductible resets to zero paid.
Copays = Visits this year × Copay per visit
Insurable amount = Total billed this year − Copays
Applied to deductible = min(Insurable amount, Remaining deductible)
Subject to coinsurance = Insurable amount − Applied to deductible
Coinsurance owed = Subject to coinsurance × Your coinsurance %
Raw patient cost = Copays + Applied to deductible + Coinsurance owed
You pay = min(Raw patient cost, Out-of-pocket maximum)
Insurance pays
Insurance pays = Total cost − Your total out-of-pocket
Assumptions
  • Copays count toward your out-of-pocket maximum but not your deductible.
  • Your deductible and out-of-pocket maximum reset on January 1st each year.
  • The concierge medical service fee is fully insurable and flows through your deductible and coinsurance.
  • Post-birth visits are spaced every 2 weeks.

This calculator provides estimates only. Coverage varies by plan. Please verify your specific benefits with your insurance provider before making financial decisions.
